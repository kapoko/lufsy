Fast, reliable loudness checks

Lufsy gives quick, reliable loudness reads for media workflows. It batch processes audio / video files and helps post-production, mastering, and broadcast teams identify non-compliant files before delivery.

Supported loudness norms

  • EBU R128
  • ATSC A/85
  • ARIB TR-B32
  • Common streaming targets and peak checks (YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and more)
  • All ITU-R BS.1770 compliant
